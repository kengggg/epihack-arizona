# The Epihack Participatory Surveillance Fake Data

The Data is Fake.\
Never been faker, than this.\
Oh, the beautiful fake data.\
No single line of this, were generated by Llama.

## What's Real (and What's Not)

The ages are real, the genders are too,\
Real symptoms, real seasons all shining through.\
The month, day, and onset-lag stay —\
Templated from Sicksense, then sent our way.

Names? FAKE. Emails? FAKE-faked!\
Zips and counties? All proportioned and staked\
To Arizona's population, true to its share,\
So Maricopa sits big and the rurals stay spare.

Years got remapped to a clean three-year strap:\
From May-twenty-one, twenty-twenty-three on the map,\
To May-twenty-one, twenty-twenty-six — snap!

## The Big Big Numbers

Five-hundred-three-thousand, eight-forty-one rows!\
One-twenty-K *sick*, three-eighty-three-K *fine*-in-their-clothes.\
Sixty percent female, forty percent male,\
Ages from zero to one-hundred — full tale!

## Geography, Oh My!

Fifteen counties, one-hundred-plus zips,\
Maricopa's sixty-six (and a piece of the chips),\
Pima's fifteen, the rurals are slim.

Area codes route just where they should —\
*602 / 480 / 623* the Valley made good,\
*520* down south where Pima folks rally,\
*928* up north, from Flag to the alley.

## The Symptoms We Saw

Cough was the chart-topper — eighteen-thousand-plus!\
Sore throat and fever climbed onto the bus,\
Then chills, body aches, diarrhea in tow.\
*Yellow_skin_yellow_eyes*? One-hundred-ten — oh-so-low.

## Who Reported?

Students lead loudest, eighty-one-thousand-plus calls,\
Then Homemakers, Cashiers, and Nurses-in-halls,\
Clerks, Drivers, Food-Service — they all came along —\
A workaday Arizona, in Sicksense's song.

## Seasons, Oh Seasons

Spring's the loud season, March-April-peak,\
Then July climbs back up (the desert is sleek).\
June's the low valley, December stays mild —\
A real-world seasonality, never beguiled.

## Outbreaks We Planted (and Caught!)

Five planted clusters — and FIVE were detected!\
By zip-week z-score, exactly expected:\
Tucson school ILI (z-seventeen-point-five),\
Phoenix foodborne GI, oh-so-alive,\
Flagstaff respiratory, up in the snow,\
Shelter Hep A — yes, even *that* one says hello!\
Valley Fever endemic, fifteen-hundred-eighty-four cases,\
In endemic counties only — zero false places.

## A Few Honest Caveats

Four orphan columns, no Sicksense to lean on:\
*difficulty_breathing*, *chills*, *smell-or-taste-gone*,\
plus *yellow_skin_yellow_eyes* — at low rates conditioned-on.

*Headache* in Sicksense has no hackathon home,\
So those rows are sick-but-symptoms-show-none.\
Tribal zips? Underweighted a smidge —\
A nod to the digital divide, not the ridge.

## Reproducing These Numbers

```bash
sqlite3 data/epihack.db \
  "SELECT county, COUNT(*) FROM incidents GROUP BY county ORDER BY 2 DESC;"
```

All tables above were generated by analogous `GROUP BY` queries against `data/epihack.db`.
