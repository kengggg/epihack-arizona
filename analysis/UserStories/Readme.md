## User Stories Guides

The document contains five sections:
* **Section 1** — Critical Analysis evaluates the 10 source templates against LLM prompt engineering best practices, identifying strengths (temporal specificity, user-centric framing) and eight substantive gaps (no One Health integration, absent equity stratification, missing uncertainty communication, underspecified LLM instructions, no tribal data sovereignty framing, among others). It concludes with an expanded six-objective functional framework.
* **Section 2** — 20 Participant Prompts cover: personal symptom trajectory, community comparison, emerging symptom alerts, heat-related risk, re-engagement, symptom co-occurrence, vaccination guidance, animal exposure screening, household cluster reporting, wastewater contextualization, loss of smell/taste, demographic risk stratification, mental health burden, occupational exposure, high-acuity triage (Rash/Bleeding), participation recognition, pediatric proxy reporting, Spanish-language adaptation, tribal community messaging, and post-recovery follow-up.
* **Section 3** — 20 Official Prompts cover: daily situation briefs, geographic cluster detection, One Health zoonotic risk assessment, health equity reports, multi-source triangulation, communications drafting, demographic shift alerts, rare symptom sentinel events, reporter bias assessment, seasonal baseline recalibration, vector-borne correlation, resource allocation, school health monitoring, agricultural worker surveillance, tribal situation reports, hypothesis generation, after-action synthesis, infodemic monitoring, ML model interpretation, and policy intelligence briefs.
* **Section 4** addresses eight implementation considerations: privacy/HIPAA, health literacy, statistical power, One Health architecture, tribal OCAP® principles, clinical safety boundaries, temporal latency, and hallucination mitigation.
* **Section 5** presents 15 clarifying questions across four domains: platform architecture, data governance, LLM configuration, and phasing priorities.
